Our Commitment to Digital Accessibility

Big Play Entertainment is committed to ensuring that our website, bigplayatl.com, is accessible to all visitors, including those with disabilities. We believe everyone should be able to explore our attractions, book parties, make reservations, and access information about our venue independently and with ease.

We are actively working to meet or exceed the requirements of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA, recognized as the industry standard for digital accessibility in 2025.

Accessibility Features

Current Accessibility Features

Our website currently includes:

  • Mobile Responsive Design: Our website adapts to all screen sizes and devices, including smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers.
  • Keyboard Navigation: You can navigate our website using only a keyboard without requiring a mouse.
  • Alternative Text: Images on our site include descriptive alt text to provide context for screen reader users.
  • Semantic HTML Structure: Our pages use proper heading hierarchies and HTML markup to support assistive technologies.
  • Clear Navigation: Consistent menus and navigation paths throughout the site.
  • Readable Fonts: Text is designed for readability with resizable options.

Features in Development

We are actively working to enhance:

  • Video Captions: Adding captions and transcripts to all video content.
  • Color Contrast Optimization: Reviewing and improving color contrast ratios across all pages to meet WCAG 2.2 standards.
  • Form Accessibility: Enhancing our online booking and contact forms for improved screen reader compatibility.
  • ARIA Labels: Implementing comprehensive ARIA (Accessible Rich Internet Applications) labels for dynamic content.

Helpful Accessibility Tips You Can Use

You don’t have to wait for us—many accessibility features are already built into your device or browser! Here are some tools and settings you can use right now to improve your browsing experience on our site:

🔍 Zoom & Text Size

On Desktop:

  • Windows: Hold Ctrl and press + to zoom in, – to zoom out
  • Mac: Hold Command and press + to zoom in, – to zoom out
  • All Browsers: Press Ctrl + 0 (Windows) or Command + 0 (Mac) to reset zoom

On Mobile:

  • iPhone/iPad: Go to Settings > Display & Brightness > Text Size, or use the Zoom feature in Settings > Accessibility
  • Android: Go to Settings > Display > Font Size and Style

📖 Screen Readers (Free)

Desktop:

  • Windows: NVDA (free, open-source)
  • Mac: VoiceOver (built-in – press Command + F5 to activate)
  • Chrome: ChromeVox extension

Mobile:

  • iPhone/iPad: VoiceOver (Settings > Accessibility > VoiceOver)
  • Android: TalkBack (Settings > Accessibility > TalkBack)

⌨️ Keyboard Navigation Tips

Navigate our website without a mouse:

  • Tab: Move forward through links and buttons
  • Shift + Tab: Move backward
  • Enter/Return: Activate links and buttons
  • Spacebar: Scroll down or activate checkboxes
  • Arrow Keys: Navigate menus and dropdowns
  • Esc: Close pop-ups or dialogs

🎨 High Contrast & Dark Mode

Windows 10/11:

  • Go to Settings > Ease of Access > High Contrast
  • Choose a high contrast theme

Mac:

  • System Preferences > Accessibility > Display > Increase Contrast
  • Enable Dark Mode in System Preferences > General

Browser Extensions:

  • High Contrast for Chrome
  • Dark Reader for Chrome or Firefox

🗣️ Text-to-Speech

Windows:

  • Narrator (built-in): Press Windows Key + Ctrl + Enter

Mac:

  • Select text and go to Edit > Speech > Start Speaking

Browser Extensions:

  • Read Aloud for Chrome/Edge
  • Natural Reader for Chrome

🖱️ Mouse & Pointer Options

Larger Cursor (Windows):

  • Settings > Ease of Access > Cursor & Pointer > Change Pointer Size

Larger Cursor (Mac):

  • System Preferences > Accessibility > Display > Cursor Size

Sticky Keys (for keyboard shortcuts):

  • Windows: Settings > Ease of Access > Keyboard
  • Mac: System Preferences > Accessibility > Keyboard

📱 Mobile Accessibility Settings

iPhone/iPad (iOS):

  • Settings > Accessibility offers dozens of features:
    • VoiceOver (screen reader)
    • Zoom
    • Display & Text Size
    • Spoken Content
    • AssistiveTouch (on-screen controls)

Android:

  • Settings > Accessibility includes:
    • TalkBack (screen reader)
    • Magnification
    • Font Size
    • Color Correction
    • Live Captions

🌐 Browser Accessibility Settings

Chrome:

  • Settings > Accessibility
  • Enable features like captions, focus highlighting, and more

Firefox:

  • Settings > General > Language and Appearance
  • Adjust fonts, colors, and zoom settings

Safari (Mac):

  • Safari > Preferences > Websites > Reader
  • Strips away clutter for easier reading

Edge:

  • Settings > Accessibility
  • Includes read aloud, high contrast, and more

🔊 Live Captions (For Videos)

Windows 11:

  • Quick Settings > Accessibility > Live Captions
  • Works system-wide for any audio

Chrome:

  • Settings > Accessibility > Live Caption
  • Auto-captions for media playing in browser

Pixel Phones (Android):

  • Settings > Accessibility > Live Caption

💡 Quick Tip: Browser Reader Mode

Most modern browsers have a “Reader Mode” that strips away ads, images, and distractions:

  • Safari: Click the Reader icon in the address bar
  • Firefox: Click the book icon in the address bar
  • Edge: Click the book icon or press F9

This can make our website much easier to read and navigate!
